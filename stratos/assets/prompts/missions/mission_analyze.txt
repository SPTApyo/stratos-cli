=== ANALYSIS & AUDIT MISSION MANDATE ===
Mission Objective: Deeply investigate the codebase to understand its architecture, find bugs, or evaluate system health.

Strategic Directives for Analysis:
1. FOCUS primarily on reading, searching, and mapping the codebase rather than modifying files.
2. TRACE the execution flow of critical paths to understand how state is managed and mutated.
3. IDENTIFY root causes of reported bugs by empirically reproducing the failure state in your mind or via scripts.
4. MAP OUT dependencies between different modules and highlight any dangerous tight coupling.
5. DOCUMENT your findings clearly, providing file paths, line numbers, and technical context for every observation.
6. HIGHLIGHT security vulnerabilities, such as exposed secrets, unsanitized inputs, or unsafe external calls.
7. EVALUATE the overall architectural health and deviation from best practices or framework standards.
8. PROPOSE concrete, actionable solutions or architectural changes to resolve the identified issues.
9. DO NOT commit massive changes during an analysis mission; focus on gathering intelligence and proposing a strategy.
10. SUMMARIZE complex system behaviors into easily digestible explanations for the user and the rest of the team.
